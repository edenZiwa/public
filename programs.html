OUR PROGRAMS

Practical expressions of compassion.

Garden of Mercy Foundation seeks to develop child-focused initiatives that respond to identified needs and create meaningful opportunities for children, families and communities.

OUR APPROACH

From compassion to practical action.

Our program approach is centered on the belief that compassion should respond to real needs while protecting the dignity, safety and rights of the people we serve.

Programs should be developed around identified needs, available resources, appropriate partnerships and responsible organizational planning.

Where programs are still being developed, this page describes intended areas of focus rather than claiming services that have not yet been formally established.

Sowing Seeds of Mercy and Compassion

Every initiative should seek to turn compassion into responsible, practical and sustainable action.

OUR PRINCIPLES

How programs should be delivered

Child-centered

Children's safety, dignity, rights and wellbeing should remain central to program decisions.

Safeguarding

Programs should operate with appropriate protection and safeguarding procedures.

Accountability

Activities and resources should be managed responsibly and monitored against appropriate objectives.

Partnership

Sustainable community impact can be strengthened through responsible collaboration.

HOW WE WORK

From identified need to responsible action

A mature program should begin with understanding a need, continue through planning and implementation, and include monitoring, accountability and learning.

01

Identify

Understand community and beneficiary needs.

02

Plan

Develop appropriate objectives, resources and safeguards.

03

Act

Deliver approved activities responsibly.

04

Learn

Monitor results, learn and improve future programs.

Responsible impact reporting

As programs develop, verified information about activities, beneficiaries, outputs and outcomes can be published through the Foundation's impact and reporting channels. We will avoid presenting unverified figures as confirmed impact.
