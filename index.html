Who we are

A foundation built around mercy.

Garden of Mercy Foundation is a child-focused foundation committed to transforming compassion into practical support.

We believe that children deserve dignity, care, opportunity and an environment where they can grow with hope for the future. Our work is centered on identifying meaningful needs and responding through responsible, compassionate action.

Mercy

Responding to genuine needs with compassion and care.

Creating opportunities that encourage a brighter future.
