ABOUT US

A foundation built around mercy.

Garden of Mercy Foundation exists to turn compassion into practical action, with a particular focus on children.

Our foundation

Garden of Mercy Foundation is a child-focused foundation established around the principles of mercy, compassion, dignity and service.

We seek to identify meaningful ways of supporting children and communities while encouraging people to participate in acts of compassion.

Our work is inspired by the teaching of Jesus in Matthew 25:35–45.

Sowing Seeds of Mercy and Compassion

Compassion becomes meaningful when it is translated into action.

OUR VALUES

What guides our work

Our approach is centered on compassion, dignity and meaningful community participation.

Compassion

We value compassionate responses to human needs and seek practical ways to extend care to children and communities.

Dignity

Every child deserves to be treated with dignity, respect and care.

Community

Meaningful change requires people and communities working together toward a more compassionate future.

OUR INSPIRATION

Matthew 25:35–45

Our foundation is inspired by the call to respond to people in need with mercy, compassion and practical service.
