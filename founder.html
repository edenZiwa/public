About the Founder

A vision that puts compassion into action.

Apostle Derrick Musinguzi is the founder of Garden of Mercy Foundation.

The Foundation’s work is guided by a commitment to serve children, vulnerable people and communities facing hardship. Its message is simple: mercy is more than a feeling; it is a willingness to recognize a need and respond with care.

Public ministry materials also associate Apostle Derrick Musinguzi with Power of Revelation Ministries and Revelation Church Kampala. These associations form part of his ministry background, while Garden of Mercy Foundation maintains its own humanitarian purpose and organizational responsibilities.

To see a need, respond with compassion and help restore hope.

From Faith to Service

A foundation built around mercy in action.

Garden of Mercy Foundation is committed to practical expressions of care for children, vulnerable people, families and communities facing hardship.

Inspired by the call to care for our neighbours, the Foundation seeks to turn compassion into meaningful action—supporting dignity, restoring hope and encouraging people to stand together when help is needed.

Our Commitment

Sowing mercy.
Restoring hope.

Compassion becomes meaningful when it moves us to act.

We believe mercy is more than a feeling—it is compassion put into action. We seek to support vulnerable individuals, children, families and communities facing hunger, poverty, sickness and hardship through practical help that respects dignity and nurtures hope.

Our mission is to see a need, respond with compassion and help transform lives. Through giving, volunteering, partnerships and community outreach, people can join in making a difference.

One act of kindness can restore hope. One helping hand can change a life.
